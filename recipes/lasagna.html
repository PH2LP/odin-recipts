Lasagna Recipt

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add ground beef, onion, and garlic; cook until meat is browned.
  4. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat mixture, ricotta cheese, mozzarella cheese, and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used.
  6. Bake for 30-40 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  7. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.
